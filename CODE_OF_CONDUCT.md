# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AgentChecksum welcomes contributors and users of every background and experience level.
Everyone participating in this project deserves a respectful environment, regardless of age,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, religion,
sexual orientation, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and give newcomers room to learn.
- Discuss ideas and code on their merits. Explain disagreements without attacking people.
- Give constructive feedback, accept corrections, and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect personal boundaries, privacy, and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Help keep discussions useful, accessible, and focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, slurs, threats, or intimidation.
- Sexual comments or imagery and unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or helps with an investigation.

## Scope

This policy applies in the project's issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and
other community spaces. It also applies when someone represents AgentChecksum publicly,
such as through an official project account or at a project event.

## Reporting a concern

Report conduct concerns privately to Furkan Turkoglu, the project maintainer, at
[furkanturkogluuu.0@gmail.com](mailto:furkanturkogluuu.0@gmail.com).
Include links or relevant context, what happened, and any immediate concerns. Share only
information needed to understand the incident; do not post private reports in public issues.

Reports will be handled discreetly. Information will be shared only as needed to investigate
and respond, and the reporter's identity will be protected where possible. The maintainer
will acknowledge reports and explain next steps as availability permits; this small project
does not promise a fixed response time.

Security vulnerabilities have a separate reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove or edit content that violates this policy and restrict participation
when needed to protect the community. Responses depend on the severity, impact, and repetition
of the behavior, and may include:

1. A request to stop the behavior and a clarification of expectations.
2. A warning with conditions for continued participation.
3. A temporary restriction from project spaces.
4. A permanent ban for serious or repeated violations.

Serious incidents may result in an immediate restriction without earlier warnings. Decisions
will be explained privately to the people involved when feasible. Anyone affected may request
reconsideration through the same private contact, with additional context. Maintainers are
also expected to follow this policy.
